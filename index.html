Baked at home, with love

Golab means rosewater in Persian, the smell of every celebration we grew up with. We bake Persian favourites like baklava cake next to the birthday cakes, cookies and pastries our friends keep asking for.

Every order is made fresh for you. Tell us the occasion and we'll help you pick the size and the flavour.

Everyone is welcome at our table. We are a Persian 2SLGBTQ+ family and proud allies. Pride is every day here.
